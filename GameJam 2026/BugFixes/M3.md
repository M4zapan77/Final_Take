# M3 physical interaction safety

- Replaced wardrobe-only prop copies with exclusive first-round physical inventory, preserving appearance IDs independently from ownership.
- Paused scene bodies and their velocities across wardrobe and actor transitions without pausing the UI/world.
- Kept stationary trajectory boundaries so compacting idle frames cannot make an object start moving early in the movie.
- Replaced physical resimulation with recorded transforms and chronological ownership events.
- Added holder clearance, CCD, bounded throw speed, collision cooldowns and out-of-area recovery.
- Made rewatching immutable and checked final prop transforms after both movie playbacks.
- Added observable robbery/noise reactions to existing simple NPC states.

The initial integration had compile-time TObjectPtr deduction errors, corrected before testing. All recorded test results refer to the subsequent successful build. No original art or Blueprint was edited.
